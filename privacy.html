Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026.

Studio is used only by the owner of the YouTube channels LastLog and Syv Ulv, on the owner's own computer.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, Studio uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to upload and schedule videos, set thumbnails and playlists, and read view, watch-time and retention statistics for the owner's own channels.

What it stores

The sign-in token and channel statistics stay on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data about viewers or other people is collected.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Studio's use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
